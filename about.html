Dear Friends,

Tis is a lil' bit about me 👋

Hi there! I’m Wendy, a product designer whose work sits at the intersection of curiosity, strategy, and empathy. I believe good design starts with the questions you ask, and I love understanding how products create real value for both the people who use them and the businesses that build them.

Currently, I am working across different product areas within Lyft Business, designing for the businesses and people who rely on Lyft to get where they need to go. These are problems with lots of moving parts: complex workflows, many different users, and real operational constraints. I love digging into that ambiguity with my teammates, asking questions until I understand the problem underneath, and then shaping it into something that feels clear and delightful.

Prior to this, I studied Business and Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon, then went on to get my master’s in Information Experience Design at Pratt. Along the way, I’ve designed for startups, a city government agency, and now big tech. But long before any of that, I was a scrappy graphic designer teaching myself Photoshop in middle school 😎

Outside of work, you can find me catching Pokemon, making posters for fun, and discovering new hikes around the Bay.

With love,
Wendy Li

How I work

  1. I always make sure I truly understand the product, user behavior, and core problems. I reach out to collaborators, dig into context, and question things that don’t quite add up.
  2. I bring curiosity to conversations and bridge perspectives across teams, weaving together viewpoints into solutions that feel bigger than what any one person could create alone.
  3. Years of graphic design have sharpened my eye to balance aesthetics with experience, helping me create work that not only looks good but also feels right to the people using it.
